- **The 3-Dial System.** Leaders control their experience with three simple dials: Nudge Frequency (from one per week to daily), Insight Depth (from a single sentence to full data with peer benchmarks), and Channel Mix (choose any combination of Slack, Teams, Zoom, email, mobile push, or in-app only). Every nudge is then personalized using Claude AI -- not generic tips, but specific prompts tied to that leader's blind spots, their upcoming meetings, and their team's actual feedback.

- **Mirror Moments.** Monthly side-by-side comparisons showing how the leader rates themselves versus how their team actually experiences them, across all five SCARF domains. This is the moment most leaders go from curious to committed.

- **The Cascade Model.** Once a leader reaches Stage 2, they can invite their direct reports to begin their own Catalyst journey. Those leaders then invite their reports. Culture change propagates downward through the organization, with an executive dashboard showing safety scores across the entire cascade tree.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

---

### External FAQs (Customer-Facing)

---

**1. What is Catalyst?**

Catalyst is an AI-powered coaching platform that helps executive leaders build psychologically safe teams through personalized micro-behavior nudges delivered in the tools they already use -- Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and email. It is not another app to check. It is not a training program. It is a layer of intelligence that sits on top of a leader's existing workflow and helps them do what they already do -- run meetings, have 1:1s, make decisions -- with one small, specific improvement at a time. The entire daily time commitment is under 30 seconds for the nudge itself, with optional deeper analytics for leaders who want them.

---

**2. How is Catalyst different from executive coaching?**

Traditional executive coaching is valuable but has three structural limitations. First, it requires 1-2 hours per month of scheduled time that executives routinely cancel. Second, skills practiced in a coaching session do not automatically transfer to the pressure of a real meeting -- context collapse means leaders revert to default behaviors when stressed. Third, there is no continuous feedback loop. A 360 review happens quarterly at best.

Catalyst solves all three. It requires zero scheduled time -- nudges arrive in the leader's existing workflow. It delivers prompts at the exact moment of relevance -- 15 minutes before a specific meeting with a specific person. And it provides a continuous feedback loop through monthly team pulse surveys and Zoom meeting analysis that shows whether behavior is actually changing how the team experiences the leader.

Catalyst costs $50-150 per leader per month. Traditional executive coaching costs $500-1,500 per hour. Catalyst is not a replacement for deep executive coaching -- it is the daily reinforcement layer that makes coaching stick.

---

**3. How much time does Catalyst require from me?**

At minimum settings: 30 seconds per week. You receive one email digest with one insight and one suggestion. That is it.

At default settings: about 30 seconds per day. You receive 2-3 nudges per week, each one a single sentence with a specific behavior to try in an upcoming meeting. You respond with one tap: "Tried it," "Skip," or "Later."

At maximum settings: under 5 minutes per day. You get daily nudges, post-meeting insights from Zoom AI Companion, the real-time in-meeting sidebar, and weekly analytics.

You control this entirely through three dials in the app. You can change your settings at any time. If you get busy, the system detects it -- if you skip three nudges in a row, Catalyst automatically goes quiet and sends a simple message: "We have quieted things down. Tap here whenever you are ready for more."

---

**4. What if I am too busy? What if it feels overwhelming?**

This is the scenario we designed for first. Executives are the most time-constrained people in any organization. The absolute worst thing Catalyst can do is feel like another obligation.

Seven safeguards are hard-coded into the system and cannot be overridden: a maximum of one nudge per day, quiet hours (no nudges before 8am or after 7pm), meeting density detection (if you have 4+ hours of back-to-back meetings, Catalyst goes silent), a cool-down period after every skip, a gradual onboarding ramp (one nudge in Week 1, two in Week 2, full cadence only by Week 4), seasonal awareness (quarter-end and board prep weeks automatically reduce to minimum), and a one-tap "Pause Everything" button that stops all nudges for one week, two weeks, or one month with no questions asked.

Missing a nudge has zero consequences. There are no streaks that shame you. When you come back, Catalyst starts gently -- a single, high-relevance nudge tied to a specific upcoming meeting. If you engage, frequency gradually returns to your previous setting.

---

**5. How does the Zoom integration work?**

Catalyst integrates with Zoom in two ways.

**Post-Meeting Insights.** After your meeting ends, Zoom's AI Companion generates a summary. Catalyst analyzes that summary for seven psychological safety signals: participation distribution (who spoke and who stayed silent), your question-to-statement ratio, whether dissent was present and acknowledged, whether contributions were attributed, whether concerns were addressed or dismissed, how much silence you allowed after questions, and whether one person dominated the agenda. You receive a single, specific coaching prompt within two hours -- for example: "You spoke for 68% of the product review. Three team members did not contribute. In your next meeting, try: 'I would love to hear from everyone -- Maya, what is your take?'"

**Real-Time In-Meeting Sidebar (optional).** You can open a Catalyst sidebar visible only to you during a live Zoom call. It provides ambient, non-distracting cues -- subtle color shifts and single-line prompts like "Pause after your next question" or "Alex has not spoken yet." You can pause it at any time with one tap. It never makes a sound. It never creates a popup. It is glanceable in under one second.

Your Zoom data is processed in real-time and then discarded. Raw transcripts are never stored. Only aggregated, anonymized safety signal analysis is retained.

---

**6. Can my team see my data? Can HR?**

No. This is non-negotiable in our architecture.

Your individual nudge responses, reflections, SCARF profile, and Mirror Moment data are never visible to anyone -- not HR, not your manager, not organization admins, not Catalyst staff. Catalyst is a coaching tool, not a surveillance tool.

Your team's pulse survey responses are anonymous. You see aggregated scores only. A minimum of three respondents is required before any results are displayed. No individual response is ever revealed.

Data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). SCARF profiles and pulse responses are encrypted with per-user keys. You can delete all your data at any time -- immediate, complete destruction, not a soft delete.

Catalyst is designed for GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2 Type II, and EU AI Act compliance.

---

**7. How does the cascade model work?**

The cascade model is how Catalyst turns individual behavior change into organizational culture change.

When you reach Stage 2 (typically around Week 7), you can invite your direct reports to begin their own Catalyst journey. They go through the same onboarding -- SCARF assessment, team pulse survey, first micro-behavior selection -- tailored to their context and their teams. When they reach Stage 2, they can invite their direct reports. And so on.

As the cascade grows, you unlock an organization dashboard showing safety scores across your entire leadership tree. You can see which teams are thriving and which are at risk -- without ever seeing any individual leader's private coaching data.

This is how culture propagates. Research shows that when a leader models psychologically safe behavior, their direct reports learn by observation and begin replicating those behaviors with their own teams. Catalyst makes this process intentional and measurable instead of leaving it to chance.

---

**8. What research is Catalyst based on?**

Catalyst is built on the convergence of five validated research streams:

- **Psychological safety research.** Amy Edmondson's foundational work (51 work teams, 1999; 136-sample meta-analysis, 22,000+ individuals). Google's Project Aristotle (180+ teams). Timothy Clark's 4 Stages framework.

- **Neuroscience.** David Rock's SCARF model and the NeuroLeadership Institute's research showing the brain processes social threats using the same circuits as physical threats (Lieberman, 2013).

- **Behavioral economics.** Thaler and Sunstein's Nudge Theory. A 2024 field experiment with 226 Danish managers and 4,442 employees proved that nudges combined with training significantly outperform training alone.

- **Habit science.** BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits (behavior = motivation + ability + prompt) and James Clear's Atomic Habits. Optimal structure: existing habit + tiny new behavior + immediate reinforcement.

- **Learning science.** The NeuroLeadership Institute's AGES model (Attention, Generation, Emotion, Spacing). Traditional training fails on three of four conditions. Catalyst's spaced, contextual, reflective approach satisfies all four.

Field validation from Humu/Perceptyx (founded by former Google CHRO Laszlo Bock) demonstrates 8-12 point improvements in manager effectiveness scores within six months using nudge-based approaches, with 95% sustained engagement.

Third, the market gap. After analyzing 19+ products in the space, no single platform combines measurement, AI coaching, nudge delivery, and cascade propagation for executive leaders. Existing tools either measure (Culture Amp, Perceptyx), coach (BetterUp, CoachHub), or nudge (Humu). None close the full loop. **9. Why React Native?**

Four reasons:

1. **Cross-platform from day one.** Executive leaders use iPhones and Android devices. Building native for both doubles development time with no corresponding user benefit. React Native + Expo gives us both platforms from a single codebase with near-native performance.

2. **The UI pattern is ideal for React Native.** Catalyst's interface is card-based -- nudge cards, SCARF assessment sliders, dashboard metrics, Mirror Moment comparisons. These are standard UI components that React Native handles well. We are not building a graphics-intensive application or a game. We are building a data-rich dashboard with clean typography and simple interactions.

3. **Fast iteration speed.** Expo's over-the-air updates let us push changes to users without App Store review cycles. For an MVP where we need to iterate quickly on nudge content, copy, and interaction patterns based on real user behavior, this is a significant advantage.

4. **Integration-ready.** The app's primary value is delivered through bots (Slack, Teams, Zoom, email), not the app itself. The app is the brain -- onboarding, settings, dashboard, Mirror Moments -- while the bots are the hands. React Native + a Node.js/Fastify backend share TypeScript across the stack, which simplifies the integration layer with bot platforms, OAuth flows, and webhook handlers.
